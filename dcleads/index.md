# DC Leads

DC Leads is a Joomla extension for lead capture forms. Every enquiry is saved to your database before any email is sent, and each lead records the ad, campaign or click that brought the visitor. File uploads are in progress.

## Features

- **Form Builder** – 16 field types, full or half width, placeholders, help text and required fields. Three starter forms are ready on install.
- **Never Lose a Lead** – Leads are stored first. If a notification email fails, the dashboard flags it and you can resend it.
- **Campaign Tracking** – UTM tags, Google and Meta click IDs, landing page and referrer, kept from the visitor's first page.
- **International Phone Field** – A country picker that checks the number as it is typed and stores it in international form.
- **Country and State** – A searchable country list, with an optional state or province list for 13 countries.
- **Background Sending** – Inline validation and a thank-you message in place. Forms still send without JavaScript.
- **Spam Protection** – Honeypot, a minimum time on the form, any Joomla captcha plugin and per-visitor limits.
- **Lead List** – Statuses, assignment, private notes, filters and CSV export.
- **Auto-reply** – An optional thank-you email to the visitor.
- **Health Checks** – The dashboard checks mail, sender, notifications, spam protection, tracking and your license.
